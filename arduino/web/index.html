        How to connect the lamp
        1. Plug the lamp into this computer with the USB cable.
        2. Close the Serial Monitor in the Arduino program (only one app can use the cable at a time).
        3. Click Connect lamp and pick USB-SERIAL CH340 or the COM port.
        4. Before uploading new code from Arduino, click Disconnect here first.

        The timer lives on the lamp: it starts when you sit down and notices when you step away. This page mirrors it and keeps your history.

        Tasks and study history are saved in this browser.
