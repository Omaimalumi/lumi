Lumi — Product Requirements

A cozy desk study companion that notices when you sit down, counts down your study time, pauses when you leave, and quizzes you on your own notes.

1. The demo session

What the proof of concept must do smoothly. All build time goes here.

2. Full feature list

Everything else the app already has, described as it works today. Stays on the page, not checked or fixed.

How the plan grew from the scope

Scope said "let the AI study buddy quiz you." Now we know exactly what that means: 5 questions, one at a time, each tagged like "Question 2 of 5 · Mitosis," graded with an explanation when you're wrong, and ending with "Quiz done: 4/5 correct (80%)." Weak spots now light up on Lumi's screen as REVIEW: MITOSIS. You moved that into the demo because "it connects the AI to the lamp."

    Features and How We'll Know They Work

    Connecting the Lamp
    • Clicking Connect lamp connects Lumi over USB, and the page shows that it's connected.
    • The focus time picked in Session settings is the one the countdown uses, on both screens.
    Noticing You (Presence Timer) the heart of Lumi
    • With nobody within about 40 cm, Lumi shows animated eyes, and the countdown doesn't run.
    • Sitting down within about 40 cm fills a bar for 5 seconds, then the countdown starts.
    • The countdown on Lumi's screen and on the web page show the same time.
    • The page's label shows "focusing" while the countdown runs.
    Pausing When You Leave
    • The moment you leave, the countdown stops, and Lumi shows "Where'd you go?" with a 10-second countdown.
    • If you come back within 10 seconds, the countdown continues right away.
    • After 10 seconds away, Lumi shows "Paused," and the page shows "paused."
    • After you sit back down for 5 seconds, the countdown continues from the same time. It doesn't restart.
    • If you stay away for 5 minutes, the session is cancelled.
    Finishing a Session
    • At 00:00, Lumi plays a tune and its screen shows "Done!"
    Focus Mode
    • Turning on Focus mode leaves only the study timer visible.
    • Leaving Focus mode brings the full page back, and the countdown keeps running.
    AI Study Buddy Quiz AI
    • Notes uploaded to the Course library can be used by the quiz.
    • Quiz me (5 questions) asks one question at a time, about the uploaded notes.
    • Each question shows its number and topic, for example "Question 2 of 5 · Mitosis."
    • After you type an answer and press Enter, the AI says whether you're correct. If you're wrong, it explains the right answer.
    • After 5 questions, it shows the score ("Quiz done: 4/5 correct (80%)"), lists weak spots, and suggests what to review next.
    Weak Spots on the Lamp AI → lamp

    Moved from "Later" into the demo, because "it connects the AI to the lamp."

    • A topic becomes a weak spot when you've missed it more often than you've gotten it right.
    • When a new weak spot appears, Lumi's screen shows REVIEW: TOPIC, for example REVIEW: MITOSIS.

    States

    Nobody thereFriendly eyes. Countdown doesn't run.
    FocusingCountdown runs on both screens. Page shows "focusing."
    AwayCountdown stops at once. "Where'd you go?", then "Paused" after 10 s. Cancelled after 5 min.
    DoneTune and "Done!" on Lumi's screen.
    AI problemNever stops the session. Timer and lamp keep working. Quiz progress isn't lost.
    Lamp unpluggedPage shows "Lost the lamp connection, plug it back in." Lumi turns off, but today's total is saved every minute, so at most a minute is lost.

    Your Product Decisions

    • Keep every section on the page and don't change the layout. The doc shows the full app, while the video stays focused.
    • Describe the extras as they are, and spend the build time on the demo.
    • Use Focus mode for the timer moments, and scroll to the Study buddy for the quiz.
    • Time away never counts: the countdown stops the moment you leave, and a session is cancelled after 5 minutes away.
    • The AI moment is the quiz.
    • Weak spots on Lumi's screen are in the demo.
    • An AI problem never stops the session, and quiz progress isn't lost.
    • You won't appear on camera. The video shows the screens and your hands.
    • Film the finish with a short custom focus time (about 1 minute), so "Done!" can be shown without waiting.
    • Lumi's screen says "Done!" (currently "Done! Break time"), not "DONE."
    • The look stays cozy, calm, and cute.

    What's In, What's Not

    What we're building (all of it exists; make it smooth)
    • Connecting the lamp and choosing the focus time
    • Friendly eyes → sitting down → 5-second bar → countdown
    • Leaving → "Where'd you go?" → pause → resume
    • Tune and "Done!" at 00:00
    • Focus mode for the timer moments
    • Uploading notes and the 5-question AI quiz
    • Weak spots shown on Lumi's screen as REVIEW: TOPIC
    • An AI problem never stopping the session
    Full Feature List (stays on the page, not checked or fixed)
    • Try demo: try the page with a pretend lamp
    • Quick start: a short setup checklist
    • Today's tasks: today's study tasks
    • Your progress: today's study time, sessions, and streak
    • Session settings: focus time (25/40/50/60 or custom) and break length
    • Note on the lamp: send a short message to Lumi's screen
    • Today's mission: a daily study goal with progress
    • Course library: upload notes (PDF, Word, or text)
    • Notebook: write and keep your own notes
    • Study buddy buttons: Explain my note, Summarize, Make flashcards, Find my weak areas
    • Break timer: starts after "Done!"
    • Session report: a short AI-written summary
    Deferred, later, and non-goals
    • Deferred: the session report ("a nice extra if there's time," only after the demo works), and the other extras.
    • Later: fitting the hardware inside a real lamp; checking and polishing the extras.
    • Non-goals: no layout changes or redesign, no new features, no fixing extras unless they get in the way of the demo.
